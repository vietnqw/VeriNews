# 📰 VeriNews — AI-Powered News Verification for Social Media

## **Overview**

**VeriNews** is a smart browser extension that combats misinformation by providing users with an instant, AI-driven verification of news posts shared on social media. By cross-referencing a post's claims against a database of trusted news sources, VeriNews delivers a clear, actionable judgment on its credibility, helping users navigate the digital news landscape with confidence.


## **The Problem: The Speed of Misinformation**

On social media, false or misleading narratives can spread faster than the truth. Out-of-context headlines, recycled images, and subtle factual distortions are difficult for the average user to spot, polluting the information ecosystem and eroding trust.


## **The Solution: A Multi-Layered AI Verification Process**

VeriNews goes beyond simple keyword matching. When a user clicks "Verify," our backend engine performs a sophisticated, multi-pronged analysis to determine a post's authenticity:

### Preliminary Check: Image Authenticity

⚠️ **Status: Not Yet Implemented**

The system is designed to analyze the post's primary image to determine the likelihood that it was generated by an AI. This provides an immediate, crucial piece of context, as synthetic media is often used to create false narratives. However, this feature is currently under development and not yet available in the system. For now, VeriNews focuses entirely on text-based claim verification.

### 1. Content Similarity Search

First, the system will search for relevant articles from our curated database of trusted news sources. It will analyze the social media post to understand its core meaning and topic, then identify the top articles from our database that are discussing the exact same event. This step provides the foundational evidence for the next verification steps, even if the articles don't use the exact same keywords as the post.

### 2. Factual Claim Alignment

Next, the system decomposes the social media post into its individual factual claims. Each claim is then meticulously checked against the content of the matched articles from Step 1. The system determines if the trusted sources Support, Contradict, or simply Do Not Mention each specific claim, pinpointing any factual discrepancies between the post and the verified news reports.

### 3. Contextual Misuse Judgment

Finally, the system performs an overall evaluation by synthesizing all the information gathered from the previous steps. It analyzes the results of both the content similarity search and the factual alignment check to determine a final credibility score and assign a single, clear, actionable verdict that summarizes the post's reliability.


## Clear & Actionable Results

VeriNews delivers one of four verdicts, based on the evaluation in Step 3:

* 🟢 **Fully Supported:** All of the post's key claims are well-supported by the trusted articles found.
* 🟡 **Partially Supported:** Some of the post's claims are supported by trusted articles, while others are unverified or contradicted.
* 🔴 **Refuted:** One or more key claims in the post are directly contradicted by the trusted articles.
* ⚪ **Not Enough Info:** There was not enough information in the trusted source database to either confirm or deny the post's claims. This can mean no relevant articles were found (Step 1) or the matched articles did not mention the post's specific claims (Step 2).

### Confidence Scoring

Each verdict is accompanied by a confidence score (0-100) representing how certain the system is in its assessment. This score is calculated by:
- Quality of retrieved articles
- Strength of claim evidence
- Agreement between sources
- Temporal alignment of information
- Completeness of claim coverage

### Note on Image Analysis

**⚠️ AI-Generated Image Detection** is listed as a feature under development. For now, VeriNews focuses entirely on text-based claim verification against trusted news sources. Image analysis capabilities will be added in a future release.


## How It Works: A Simple User Experience

1.  A user sees a news post on their social media feed.
2.  VeriNews automatically injects a discreet **"Verify"** button into the post.
3.  The user clicks the button.
4.  Within 2-5 seconds, the backend engine performs the three-step verification process:
    - Content Similarity Search (9-stage retrieval pipeline)
    - Factual Claim Alignment (NLI-based stance classification)
    - Contextual Judgment (verdict aggregation and explanation)
5.  A results panel appears showing:
    * **A Final Verdict:** A clear, color-coded label (e.g., 🟢 Fully Supported, 🔴 Refuted, 🟡 Partially Supported, ⚪ Not Enough Info).
    * **A Confidence Score:** A numeric percentage (0-100) representing the system's certainty in the verdict.
    * **The Reason:** A Vietnamese explanation of why the post received this verdict, broken down by individual claims.
    * **Relevant Articles:** A list of trusted news articles found by the retrieval system, with highlighted sections showing relevant evidence.
    * **Claim-by-Claim Breakdown:** Details on how each extracted claim was evaluated against the found articles.


## **Impact**

VeriNews empowers users to instantly assess the reliability of online news, fighting misinformation at the source. It promotes critical thinking and responsible information sharing, contributing to a more informed and resilient online community.

## **Implementation Status**

### Implemented Features ✅
- **Content Similarity Search**: Full 9-stage retrieval pipeline with vector search (pgvector) and BM25 keyword search
- **Factual Claim Extraction**: LLM-based extraction of individual claims from social media posts
- **Factual Claim Alignment**: NLI-based stance classification (SUPPORTS, REFUTES, NOT_ENOUGH_INFO)
- **Contextual Judgment**: Verdict aggregation (FULLY_SUPPORTED, PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED, REFUTED, NOT_ENOUGH_INFO)
- **Confidence Scoring**: Multi-signal validation with 5+ weighted factors
- **Vietnamese Explanations**: Generated explanations in Vietnamese with claim-by-claim breakdown
- **Results Caching**: 24-hour Redis cache for repeated queries
- **Browser Extension**: Chrome/Chromium-based extension with content detection and verification UI
- **Multi-Source Verification**: 100+ RSS feeds from 3 Vietnamese news sources (Thanh Niên, Tuổi Trẻ, Lào Cái)

### Planned Features 🚀
- **Image Authenticity Detection**: AI-generated image detection (under development)
- **Authentication & User Accounts**: User management and personalization (Phase 3)
- **Extension UI Improvements**: Enhanced results panel and user interface (Phase 4)
- **Production Deployment**: Scalable deployment infrastructure (Phase 5)
